# Logistics Job Details

**Purpose:** State-driven job controls

## Show
- Order/crop/quantity
- Pickup
- Delivery
- Farmer/Buyer summaries
- Status
- Current valid action only

## Click / Navigation
- Available → Claim
- Claimed/Fee rejected → Propose Fee
- Advance paid → Confirm Pickup
- Pickup confirmed → Tracking
- OTP pending → Enter OTP

## Flow
- Jobs/Active → Job Details

## Important
- First eligible Logistics to claim wins atomically.

## Visual Direction
- Android-first; use approved FarmPrism Farmer Home/My Farm as the visual master.
- Cream/green/earthy palette, rounded white cards, soft shadows, clean icons, large touch targets.
- Use existing FarmPrism logo/artwork language; avoid generic admin-dashboard styling.

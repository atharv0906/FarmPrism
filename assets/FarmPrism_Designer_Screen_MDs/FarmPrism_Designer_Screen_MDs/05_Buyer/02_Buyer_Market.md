# Buyer Market

**Purpose:** Browse Auctions and Fixed Price listings

## Show
- Auction/Fixed tabs
- Crop artwork
- Crop
- Grade
- Quantity
- Reserve/fixed price
- Farmer
- Trust Score
- Time remaining

## Click / Navigation
- Auction → Auction Details
- Fixed → Fixed Details
- Market Insights → Market Prices

## Flow
- Buyer Home/bottom nav → Market

## Visual Direction
- Android-first; use approved FarmPrism Farmer Home/My Farm as the visual master.
- Cream/green/earthy palette, rounded white cards, soft shadows, clean icons, large touch targets.
- Use existing FarmPrism logo/artwork language; avoid generic admin-dashboard styling.

# Farmer Insights

**Purpose:** Quick market view for three crops

## Show
- Tomato/Onion/Potato cards
- Current/modal price
- Min/Max
- Simple trend/outlook
- Observed/source note

## Click / Navigation
- Crop → Crop Market Details
- Market Prices → Market Prices

## Flow
- Bottom nav Insights → Insights Home

## Important
- No raw daily history list.

## Visual Direction
- Android-first; use approved FarmPrism Farmer Home/My Farm as the visual master.
- Cream/green/earthy palette, rounded white cards, soft shadows, clean icons, large touch targets.
- Use existing FarmPrism logo/artwork language; avoid generic admin-dashboard styling.

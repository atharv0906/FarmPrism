# Price Insight

**Purpose:** Show only decision-ready pricing information

## Show
- Crop + Farmer Declared Grade
- Current Market Price
- Min
- Max
- Suggested Selling Price
- Next 7 Days range
- Small source note
- Short disclaimer

## Click / Navigation
- Choose Selling Method → Choose Method

## Flow
- Quality → Price Insight → Choose Method

## Important
- Do NOT show raw 30/60/90 history, confidence, volatility or long reasoning. Live API improves accuracy.

## Visual Direction
- Android-first; use approved FarmPrism Farmer Home/My Farm as the visual master.
- Cream/green/earthy palette, rounded white cards, soft shadows, clean icons, large touch targets.
- Use existing FarmPrism logo/artwork language; avoid generic admin-dashboard styling.

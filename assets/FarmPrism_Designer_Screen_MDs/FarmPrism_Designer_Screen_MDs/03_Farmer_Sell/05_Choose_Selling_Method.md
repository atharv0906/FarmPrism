# Choose Selling Method

**Purpose:** Choose Auction or Fixed Price

## Show
- Auction card — “Let buyers compete with bids”
- Fixed Price card — “Set the price you want”

## Click / Navigation
- Tap Auction card → Create Auction
- Tap Fixed Price card → Create Fixed Price

## Flow
- Price Insight → Choose Method → Auction OR Fixed Price

## Visual Direction
- Android-first; use approved FarmPrism Farmer Home/My Farm as the visual master.
- Cream/green/earthy palette, rounded white cards, soft shadows, clean icons, large touch targets.
- Use existing FarmPrism logo/artwork language; avoid generic admin-dashboard styling.
